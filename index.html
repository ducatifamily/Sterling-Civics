ABOUT OUR PROGRAM

Democracy starts with us.

Civics is the study and practice of citizenship, government, rights and responsibilities. At Sterling, juniors participate in Generation Citizen, an approach to civic learning that connects classroom knowledge with real community issues. This year, we are putting our ideas into action through student-led projects.

Learn

Understand civic responsibility and community needs.

Lead

Practice democratic decision-making and shared accountability.

Serve

Turn our learning into meaningful community action.

Every voice matters.
