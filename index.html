ABOUT OUR PROGRAM

Democracy starts with us.

Civics is the study and practice of citizenship, government, rights and responsibilities. At Sterling, juniors participate in Generation Citizen, an approach to civic learning that connects classroom knowledge with real community issues. This year, we are putting our ideas into action through student-led projects.

Learn

Understand civic responsibility and community needs.

Lead

Practice democratic decision-making and shared accountability.

Serve

Turn our learning into meaningful community action.

Student governance

Our class constitution

A framework for respect, democratic participation and responsibility.

“We, the Students of Civics 26-27, as one class, are obligated to uphold respect for one another, uphold a fair and free democracy in which all participate, and strive for responsibility and workmanship, making a positive impact on our community.”

01 / Propose

Submit a written proposal with two supporting reasons and a seconding classmate.

02 / Discuss

Indicate initial positions on a 1–5 scale and discuss respectfully to seek consensus.

03 / Decide

If consensus is not reached, use a physical ballot. Closely divided proposals are revisited.

04 / Act

Committees carry out approved projects. Officers and an advisor support accountability.

Student rights

Equal voting weight, freedom to express or oppose ideas, protection from intimidation and discrimination, and the right to propose amendments. Sensitive personnel matters use anonymous ballots.
